How it works

The front is the case exactly as it is in the cases book. Think of the diagnosis and your answers, then tap to flip: the back gives the diagnosis and the answer to every question on your own Z-book pages, cut down to only what the cases book answer mentions, with an Additional answer from cases box at the bottom for anything extra the cases book adds. Filter by chapter, and by stars with Topics. Cancelled cases are left out.
